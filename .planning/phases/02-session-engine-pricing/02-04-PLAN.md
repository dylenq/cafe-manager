    Settings: grace 2 min, warnings 10,5,1; prepaid 30 min on a started station unless stated; clock is the factory FakeTimeProvider.
    - Before any threshold: EvaluateAsync records nothing.
    - At remaining 10:00 exactly (advance 20 min) a WarningReached "10 min remaining" event appears once; evaluating again at the same time and a minute later adds none; 5 and 1 appear once each at their times.
    - Starting a 5-minute prepaid session: the first evaluation records the 10 and 5 warnings together (both already crossed), 1 later.
    - Pause during the warning window: after 10 and 5 warned, pause at remaining 4:00, advance 1 hour, evaluate: no new events, status stays Paused; resume, advance 3 min: only the 1-minute warning is recorded, 10 and 5 are not repeated.
    - Expiry: advance 30 min, evaluate: Status Grace, Expired event with AtUtc exactly the expiry instant, GraceStartedAtUtc equal to it, the open segment EndUtc equal to it, ChargedAmount unchanged; evaluating again adds no second Expired event.
    - Expiry detected late: advance 30 min + 45 s before evaluating: Expired AtUtc is still start+30min (not now), grace remaining is 75 s.
    - Grace elapsed: advance 2 more minutes from expiry and evaluate: Status Ended, EndReason Expired, EndedAtUtc equal to expiry + grace, events GraceElapsed then ExpiryActionFired recorded in that order, a fake IExpiryActionHandler received exactly one call with the station, and a second evaluation does not call it again.
    - Server-down restart: advance 3 hours with no evaluation (session Active), then one evaluation: the session ends Expired in that single call with events Warning(s), Expired (AtUtc = true expiry), GraceElapsed (AtUtc = expiry + grace), ExpiryActionFired, handler called once. Same for a session already persisted in Grace whose grace elapsed while down.
    - Grace of 0 minutes: Expired and GraceElapsed resolve in the same evaluation.
    - Settings changed between ticks (grace 2 to 5) apply on the next evaluation.
    - Extend during Grace before grace elapses (use SessionService.ExtendAsync): evaluator then sees an Active session and does not end it; after the extension runs down it expires again with a new Expired event.
    - Re-arm: after an extension lifts remaining above a previously fired threshold, that threshold records WarningReached again when crossed (only warnings recorded after the latest Extended event count as already fired).
    - Open-ended sessions never receive warnings or expiry even after 10 hours; paused prepaid sessions never expire after 10 hours.
    - Handler isolation: two handlers where the first throws: the second is still called, ExpiryActionFired is recorded with Details mentioning the failure, the session is still Ended, and EvaluateAsync does not throw.
    - Per-session isolation: if one session's processing throws (simulate with a handler or a corrupted row), other sessions in the same evaluation are still processed.
    - Notifier: Changed is raised for the station on warning, expiry and grace-elapsed; not raised when nothing changed.
    - Stale version: a concurrent staff Stop between load and save causes the evaluator to skip that session without exception and without duplicate events.

Raise SessionNotifier for each station whose state changed. No DateTime.Now/UtcNow; only the injected TimeProvider. ## Trust Boundaries

|----------|-------------|
| server clock to billing/expiry | wrong or jumping time would expire sessions early or never |
| expiry engine to device handlers (Phase 3/4 seam) | handlers act on physical machines |

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-----------|-----------------|
| T-02-18 | Denial of Service | ticker crashes on one bad session or handler | high | mitigate | per-session and per-handler try/catch; loop-level catch; tests for isolation and continuation |
| T-02-19 | Tampering | expiry missed after server downtime | high | mitigate | evaluate once at startup, true expiry instants, same-pass resolution of Grace; restart tests |
| T-02-20 | Tampering | handler invoked twice, or never | high | mitigate | session committed as Ended before handlers run; Version concurrency token; idempotency tests (repeat evaluations) |

| T-02-23 | Denial of Service | real clock ticker disturbing tests / double ticker | low | mitigate | Sessions:RunTicker flag; single hosted service registration |
