# Phase 5 Plan 01: Customer balance tracer and mode sweep Summary

## What was built

- Schema: Customers (unique Phone), TimePackages (NOCASE unique name), CustomerMinuteLedger, PackageSales, Payments, Session.CustomerId/IsMemberPricing, StationType.MemberHourlyRate, RateRule.AppliesTo. One migration generated by `dotnet tool run dotnet-ef`; later plans must not add migrations.
- `PackageService.SellAsync` writes PackageSale, Payment and the ledger credit in one SaveChanges; a failing save leaves zero rows (tested).
- `SessionService.StartFromBalanceAsync` re-reads usable minutes inside `BalanceLock`, writes session and debit rows in one SaveChanges; two concurrent 40 min starts on a 60 min balance leave exactly one success (tested).
- `StopAsync` refunds unused whole minutes for Balance sessions and records "refunded N min" in the Stopped event.
- `SessionCardModel` gained trailing `CustomerName` and `IsMemberPricing`.
- `SessionModeExtensions.IsTimed` replaced every countdown-meaning `== SessionMode.Prepaid`; cards and the sessions list show a "Balance" label.
