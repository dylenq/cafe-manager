|----------|-------------|

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-06-11 | Denial of Service | printer hangs or unreachable | medium | mitigate | 5s connect/write timeout, failure returned as PrintResult, print is outside the payment transaction so it can never fail a payment |
| T-06-12 | Tampering | ESC/POS control-byte injection via product or customer names | medium | mitigate | encoder normalises control characters to space and encodes only CP437 text; only LineFeed emits 0A; byte-level tests |

| T-06-15 | Tampering | native winspool call failure/crash | low | mitigate | OperatingSystem.IsWindows() guard, try/catch, handle closed in finally |
| T-06-SC | Tampering | npm/pip/cargo installs | high | mitigate | no package installs; own encoder instead of an ESC/POS NuGet package; csproj untouched (acceptance criterion) |
